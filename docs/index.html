01 · Live against devnet

Pick an attack

Each button builds a real transaction and asks Solana devnet to simulate it against the deployed program. A simulation runs the actual program on the actual accounts but moves nothing, so what comes back is the program's own answer. No wallet, no signature, no cost.

02 · Don't trust us, rebuild it

The shop's takings, from the chain

The program keeps a running total on each shop's account, and every payment also writes a BillPaid event. This reads both straight from devnet in your browser and checks they agree. It is the same code as rajista-pay verify.

03 · How a sale works

Six steps, one rule

01

Ring it up in naira

The cashier sells as usual in Rajista and taps USDC.

Live in Rajista
02

Price it

Naira per USDC from the Quidax USDT/NGN market, corrected by Pyth's USDC and USDT prices on Solana.

Live
03

Post the bill

The till signs a bill with its own key. The program checks the till is one the owner allowed and the USDC matches the naira price.

Live on devnet
04

Scan and pay

The customer scans a Solana Pay QR with Phantom or any wallet. Rajista pays the network fee; the customer needs USDC only.

Live on devnet
05

Money to the owner

The program moves exactly the bill's USDC into the owner's wallet, once, before the bill runs out.

Live on devnet
06

Read back, then record

The till reads the paid bill from Solana and only then records the sale, with the transaction on the receipt.

Live in Rajista

04 · What the program refuses

The rule

OnceA paid bill can never be paid again.
ExactlyThe USDC must equal the naira price at the recorded rate, rounded up.
USDC onlyA shop takes USDC; any other token is refused.
To the ownerOnly into the payout the owner's own wallet set. A cashier cannot swap in theirs.
On timeA bill lasts at most 15 minutes, because its rate goes stale.
Allowed tillsOnly a till the owner registered can post a bill. Only the owner can change the payout.

Each rule has a test named after it, run on every push: 19 program tests in LiteSVM and 12 SDK tests. See the CI runs.
