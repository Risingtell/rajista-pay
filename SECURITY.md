# Security

Rajista Pay moves money, so reports are welcome and taken seriously.

**Please do not open a public issue for a security problem.** Use GitHub's private reporting instead: the **Security** tab of this repository, then **Report a vulnerability**. Include:

- what you found and where (file and line, or the transaction),
- how to reproduce it, ideally against devnet,
- what an attacker could do with it.

The program is on devnet only; no real funds are at risk there.
